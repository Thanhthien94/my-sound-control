# My Sound Control 0.1.0 Preview 1

First independent macOS audio mixer preview. Apple Silicon, macOS 15+.

- Vietnamese menu-bar app and mixer window.
- System audio device selection and supported hardware volume/mute.
- Opt-in application mixer: 0–100% volume, mute, output meter and separate output selection.
- Favorites and local settings. Audio capture starts only when you enable Mixer; sessions are not auto-restored on launch.

## Validation

Eight DSP tests pass. On the development Mac, a test audio process ran through a 44.1 kHz device-scoped tap; output meter changed from −50 dBFS at 100% to −56 dBFS at 50%, and −∞ on mute. Switching that test process from Lenovo headphones to MacBook speakers kept the pipeline running. Stopping removed the app's aggregate from device inventory.

These observations do not establish acoustic quality, measured latency or reliability across hardware. Sleep/wake, device disconnects, a fresh permission prompt, browser helper grouping and installation on another Mac remain unverified. Some hardware has no writable master-volume property and shows a disabled system-volume slider.

## Limitations

Float32 stereo capture and mono/stereo output with matching sample rates. Duplex or multichannel devices may be rejected. No EQ, boost, Audio Unit plugins, hotkeys, timed mute or multi-device AirPlay yet.

The preview is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**. See INSTALL.md for the per-app Open Anyway flow; do not disable Gatekeeper/SIP. DMG and ZIP contain the same app; compare the asset with SHA256SUMS.txt.

SoundSource libraries/assets are not bundled. This is an independent application.
